# A Little Date With You ♡ (Interactive Date Invitation)

Interactive date invitation designed for **Mau** from **Badak**.

## ✨ Features & Flow

1. **Screen 1: Envelope Cover**
   - Clean top bar: `A LITTLE DATE WITH YOU ♡`
   - Glowing card with an adorable Flork holding flowers 💐
   - Heading: *"Hey Mau. I have a question."*
   - Subtitle: *"Just you and me. What do you say?"*
   - Button: *"Open your invitation ♡"*

2. **Screen 2: "Will you go on a date with me?"**
   - Heading: *"Will you go on a date with me?"*
   - Flork sticker changes on every single attempt with a playful pop animation (flowers 💐, big heart ❤️, blushing smile 😊, shy panic 🙈, finger heart 🫶, cheer 🎉).
   - "Nope" button dodges to diverse, widely separated positions across the lower evasion zone.
   - Natural boyfriend-style teasing captions on each try.
   - "Yes, please! ♡" button steadily grows larger on each evasion.
   - *"Not this time"* opens the cute pleading modal with a centered confirmation button.

3. **Screen 3: "You said yes!" Celebration**
   - Heart & sparkle confetti burst
   - Celebrating doodle: *"You said yes!"*
   - *"Let's pick a day →"*

4. **Screen 4: Step 1 — "When are you free?"**
   - Calendar date picker + quick chips (*"Tomorrow"*, *"This Friday"*, *"This Saturday"*, *"Next Weekend"*).

5. **Screen 5: Step 2 — "What's our vibe?" (4 Options Only)**
   - ☕ **Café**
   - 🎬 **Movie**
   - 🎮 **Gaming**
   - 🎲 **To be decided**

6. **Screen 6: Step 3 — Boarding Pass & One-Click RSVP**
   - **Ticket Card**: `Mau & Badak` with Date, Plan, and Time.
   - **Love Note**: *"▶ Open my little love note ♡"* unfolds Badak's personal note:
     > *"Every single day with you is special, but I really want to take you out on a proper date. Whether we're going to a café, watching a movie together, or just simply talking to each other, spending time with you is honestly my favorite part of life. I really can't wait to see you, my love. ♡"*
   - **Time Selector**: Clean time picker.
   - **Instant Replies**:
     - 💬 **WhatsApp**: Prepopulates full RSVP message to Badak at `+918552852791`.
     - 📸 **Instagram**: Automatically copies formatted RSVP text to clipboard and opens Instagram Direct.

## 🚀 How to Open & Host

- **GitHub Pages**: Go to repository Settings > Pages > Select `main` branch > `/ (root)` > Save. Your site will be live instantly on `https://aaryan2107.github.io/a_little_date_with_you/`.
- **Standalone Offline**: Double-click `invitation_standalone.html` to open the 100% self-contained file in any browser without needing external assets or an internet connection.
- **Local Dev**: Open `index.html` in any modern web browser.
